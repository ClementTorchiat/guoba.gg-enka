# Features 
## Relative Artifact Score
- To make individual artifact scores easier to read, they have been normalized to a maximum relative score of 100. This makes interpretation simpler: the closer the score is to 100, the closer the artifact is to mathematical perfection.
- This does not affect the actual/raw score from the original system, which is still calculated in the background. You can view that number by hovering your mouse over an artifact's score. Hovering over an artifact's score also reveals that this new score display has a cap of 100 to avoid any confusion.
## Team Recommendations (Roadmap)

- **Algorithmic generation of top teams**: The engine now analyzes all the characters in your showcase to identify your best "Main DPS" units. It then instantly recommends the 3 highest-performing teams built around these characters, based on your builds' efficiency percentage relative to their theoretical maximum score.

- **Strict team independence option**: Integration of a "No duplicates" mode. With a single click, the algorithm recalculates all recommendations to propose 3 teams guaranteed to share no characters.

- **Dynamic synergy with the roadmap focus**: The module is fully integrated with the page's "Focus" filter. Selecting a specific character (whether DPS, Sub-DPS, or Support) forces the algorithm to re-evaluate its suggestions and prioritize a high-performing team that includes that character, allowing you to instantly find the best composition for your favorite unit.
- **Hall of Fame:** Added a new module showcasing the account's best pieces based on score or crit value.

## Changes
- Updated location badge colors (EU, NA, CN, Asia).
- Updated colors and borders for AR and achievement badges.
- Updated the layout of recent accounts on the home page.
- Updated the icon for the button to access the account roadmap.
- Swapped "**Team**" and "**Patch Notes**" in the navigation sidebar.
- The modules in the "Analysis and tips" and "Account Roadmap" sections now follow a strict grid, regardless of screen or sidebar size.
- Improved overall site accessibility: added localized accessible names (`aria-label`) to icon-only buttons (search button, help button, tutorial close button) and added missing alternative text (`alt`) to certain dynamic images (player avatar, homepage score icon).
- **Account Roadmap and Gear Audit Update**
	- Visual overhaul of the Account Roadmap for a cleaner, more editorial look (removing dark blocks, increasing spacing, adjusting contrast, and using fine separator lines), including replacing the old horizontal character selection list with a compact drop-down menu. 
	- Full layout standardization between the Hall of Fame and the Weakest Link Audit to create a streamlined design, removing large avatars and rank numbers in favor of displaying the character thumbnail beneath the gear piece details. 
	- Sorting system enhancements: integration of the "Overall Score / Crit Value" filter in the Weakest Link Audit (to quickly identify gear pieces lacking offensive stats) and setting "Overall Score" as the default sort mode for the Hall of Fame (to better reflect build balance).
