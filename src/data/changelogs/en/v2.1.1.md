# Features 
## Leaderboards
- **Global Leaderboard System:** Full implementation of leaderboard pages allowing you to compare your character builds with those of players worldwide.
- Integration of a Supabase database to persistently store stat calculations and character builds.
- Leaderboard filtering based on Energy Recharge thresholds.
- Leaderboards feature asynchronous background updates to ensure the data displayed remains current and accurate.
- **Data Security via Hono API:** Integration of the Hono router acting as a server-side firewall and validator. Character scores and builds are calculated and verified by the server before being inserted into Supabase, making it impossible for users to cheat or tamper with statistics.

## Authentication and Profiles
- **Discord Login**: Added support for Discord authentication for user accounts.
- Logging in via Discord allows you to :
	- Claim ownership of an UID.
	- Store locally more characters than just those displayed in the in-game showcase.
		- *Note: The long-term goal is to be able to link any UID to all of its leaderboard entries for any user, without requiring a Discord login; however, this would necessitate an infrastructure capable of handling a massive volume of database queries — something the site does not (yet) support.*
	- Unlock detailed build comparisons with other players. If both you and the person you are inspecting own a particular character, a new comparison module will appear in the overview.
	- Change the score display in the profile view sidebar.
	- Request the deletion of build data from the database (and consequently from the leaderboards).
	- Adjust UI transparency settings for better readability.

## Stat Chart
- Active buffs have been moved within the interface (see further down in the patch notes). A spider chart has been added in their place.
- This chart allows you to visualize the ratio between base stats and combat stats at a glance.
- Hovering your mouse over one of the chart's vertices displays the specific stat and its value.

## Detailed Build Comparison
- You can now directly compare your character with that of a player you are inspecting, provided you have a Discord account linked to your UID.
- A radar chart appears and dynamically adjusts to overlay your build's stats (in a dashed blue line) against the inspected build's, visually highlighting their respective strengths and weaknesses.
- A new panel lists the specific differences for each stat (positive differentials in green, negative in red), allowing for an instant assessment of how your build compares.
- The view also provides a concise summary of the differences in equipped weapons, artifact set bonuses, and constellation levels between your character and theirs.

## Weekly Farming Schedule
- A weekly talent-farming schedule has been added to the "Action Plan" section of the Account Review.
- Weapon materials are also included.
- This allows you to plan your daily Resin usage in advance and anticipate your Mora expenditures.
- Intelligent detection of level thresholds (e.g., Ascension 6 / Level 80 required for talents 9 and 10).
- **Pre-farming mode & boss counters**: If a talent is temporarily locked due to your character's ascension level, an alert badge will appear, showing the exact quantity of world boss materials and regional specialties you need to collect to unlock it.

## Best Teams
- Improved the calculation engine for "best teams" within the account review. The engine will now account for all teams across all your DPS characters, rather than just the single best one.
- Added an option to restrict team compositions to characters you actually own (this works in conjunction with the local saving of your character roster).

# UX/UI
## Your character, your sheet
- **Full character sheet customization**: a new panel located to the right of the character sheet allows for complete customization.
- **Classic & Modern Modes**: switch with a single click between the familiar look and an all-new Modern mode, which completely reimagines the overall design for a cleaner, more impactful aesthetic (give it a try, I promise it’s worth it!).
- **Detailed settings** : you can adjust the image, color, transparency, brightness, borders, shadows, and much more!
- The goal is to enable you to export a character sheet that perfectly matches your personal taste.

## Additions
- Added a summary of top rankings to the account review.
- Added new seasonal decorative elements (including site anniversary themes) to style the interface according to the time of year.
- Added logos of the technologies used to the footer and new icons for the overall interface.
- Added stat icons to the equipped weapon card in the character sheet.
- **Teammate Details & Team Buffs**
	- Simply hovering over a teammate now displays their detailed profile in real time. 
	- It shows the recommended weapon, suggested artifact set, ideal constellation level, and all buffs currently applied to the character being inspected. 
	- The goal is to provide full visibility into the source of these buffs and to help guide you in optimizing your teams.
- **Realistic Team Sequencing & Targeted Buffs**
	- **Canonical Positioning:** Gone are the days when the character being viewed was always relegated to the first position! Teams now appear in their actual order.
	- The character currently being viewed remains instantly identifiable thanks to a distinctive glowing border, regardless of whether they are in the first or last position.
	- This sequencing is applied throughout the site: in the character profile toolbar, leaderboard previews, and tooltips within the Account Review.
	- **Intelligent Directional Buffs:** The calculation engine now accounts for a character’s exact position within the rotation. Single-target effects, such as the ATK% boost from **Thrilling Tales of Dragon Slayers**, now apply only to the character in slot 1, preventing the erroneous assignment of buffs to support characters when viewing their profiles.
	- The goal is to make team compositions instantly recognizable and to further refine the accuracy of your simulated statistics.

## Changes
- **Relocation of active buffs**
	- Previously located at the bottom right of the character sheet, they are now positioned directly beneath it, above the "Analysis and tips" section. 
	- Active buffs are split into two groups: resonances first, followed by team characters. 
	- Each character buff card features background splash art and a color-coded card matching the character's own sheet to add personality, much like the character sheet itself. 
	- The goal of this change is to provide a more comprehensive and accessible view of the buffs.
- **Optimization of the Dungeon Planner & Mystic Synthesis Advisor**
	- Needs assessment now focuses exclusively on optimal sets and automatically excludes characters whose gear pieces have already reached an A+ grade with strong main stats. 
	- Each character now features a status indicator below their name, showing their current state (*Optimized*, *Set not equipped*, or identifying specific weak pieces, such as *Flower* or *Sands*). 
	- Hovering over a character opens an interactive, real-time profile displaying their active build, team composition, and the ideal gear pieces they require. 
	- The Dungeon Planner now flags "trap dungeons", which are those with an asymmetrical loot pool where one set is also available via the Mystic Synthesis system under an *Alternative Synthesis* label, while highlighting domains that offer superior, dual-value returns. 
	- The Synthesis Advisor intelligently categorizes sets and provides dynamic, context-aware guidance to help you get the most value out of recycling your relics. 
	- A scope selector allows you to instantly filter the display, letting you focus solely on the pieces that need the most attention. 
	- The goal is to provide a precise, surgical overview of your top-priority upgrades, ensuring you never waste Resin on dungeons or characters that are already fully optimized.
- **Visual overhaul of the maturity barometer cards**
	- Previously, the 5 maturity barometers in the account review provided useful advice, but only through long blocks of text that were truncated by the interface. 
	- To improve readability and accessibility, the barometer cards have undergone a complete visual redesign, highlighting key information at a single glance.
- New appearance for the account badges.
- Visual update for the Cross-Swap Optimizer.
