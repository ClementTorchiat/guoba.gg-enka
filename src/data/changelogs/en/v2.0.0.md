# Features

## Strategic Account Roadmap

- **Roadmap & Global Optimization Planner**: New comprehensive strategic decision-making tool that analyzes your entire showcase to prioritize your resin spending and maximize your characters' power.
    
- **Global Maturity Barometer**: Synthetic assessment of account progress (out of 100) calculated from 5 key gauges: weapon level (90), character Ascension (90), recommended talent levels, correct main stats, and artifact set completion.
    
- **5-Tier Action Plan (Quick Wins)**: List of concrete, immediate actions to reach 100% on each maturity indicator (automatic detection of HP%, DEF%, and EM scaling and elemental reactions for Ascension, talent priorities, Sands/Goblet/Circlet corrections, and 4-piece set completion).
    
- **Cross-Swap Optimizer**: Algorithm that detects direct artifact swaps between the characters in your showcase, generating an immediate net score gain at **0 resin** without disrupting the lending character's build.
    
- **Domain Planner**: Smart ranking of artifact Domain profitability based on your characters' actual needs, highlighting doubly profitable Domains (where both sets are usable).
    
- **Strongbox Advisor**: Targeted recommendations of which sets to recycle at the Strongbox to secure your builds without wasting resin on outdated or low-value Domains.
    
- **Sanctifying Elixir Crafting Advisor**: Decision-support module for the Artifact Transmuter that evaluates the return on investment (ROI) of your custom crafts. The algorithm calculates the expected score at +20 (with 2 guaranteed elite substats) weighted by Elixir cost (1 to 4) and the natural rarity of each piece (Goblet > Sands/Circlet > Flower/Plume), includes a budget filter, and estimates the resin saved.
    
- **"All game characters" toggle**: Extended view on the Domain Planner and Strongbox Advisor, letting you see, in a subdued display, off-showcase characters who also benefit from the farm or craft.
    
- **Weak Link Audit**: Diagnosis of the weakest artifact pieces holding back your account's performance, with identification of easy-to-replace off-set pieces and a precise estimate of the resin and farming days needed to obtain a replacement.
    
- **Interactive Character Focus system**: Select a specific character to instantly reorganize every Roadmap module and bring their potential gains, weak pieces, priority Domains, recommended Elixir crafts, and dedicated Strongbox sets to the front.
    

## Interactive Tooltip System

- **Detailed breakdown of base and combat stats**: Hovering over a stat now shows a full tooltip explaining exactly where its value comes from: the split between character base and weapon base, the total percentage bonuses (artifact main/substats, weapon bonus, Ascension, and 2-piece set bonus), and flat bonuses (Flower, Plume, and substats).
    
- **Instant artifact roll breakdown**: Hovering over a stat line on an artifact card instantly reveals the precise breakdown of each of its rolls, letting you see the distribution of upgrades at a glance.
    
- **Contextual cross-highlighting**: Hovering over a stat or a buff automatically lights up every corresponding line on the artifact cards and in the buff panel with a soft visual glow tinted to the character's signature color (`var(--char-hex)`).
    
- **Clearer buff sources and team synergies**: Hierarchical, contextualized display of active buffs that clearly states their origin (equipped weapon, set passive, or teammate — including the required Constellation, e.g. C3 or C6).
    

## Discord Sharing & Integration (OpenGraph)

- **Dynamic Discord & social media embeds**: Sharing a character link now generates a rich, personalized, contextual preview.
- **Automatic title**: Displays `[Character] by [Username]` (EN) or `[Username]'s [Character] — guoba.gg` (FR).
- **Detailed description**: Directly shows the archetype (`Freeze DPS`, `Vaporize DPS`...), level, Constellations, equipped weapon with its refinement rank (R1 to R5), key combat stats (ATK/HP/DEF, CRIT Rate, CRIT DMG, ER, EM), and active artifact set bonuses (4p / 2p+2p).
- **Official HD artwork**: Automatic use of the official splash art or the equipped outfit instead of the generic static banner.
- **Live URL sync**: The browser's address bar updates in real time as you select a character (`/?uid=...&char=...&lang=...`), making a simple copy-paste of the link instantly Discord-embed-compatible.

- **Smoother navigation**: Fully eliminated interface micro-stutters when loading account data, thanks to asynchronous processing off the main thread.

# Performance

## Additions

- **Brotli & Gzip pre-compression at build time (`vite-plugin-compression`)**: All HTML, JS, CSS, and JSON files are now pre-compressed to drastically cut bandwidth (e.g., `rollTable.json` compressed to **~9.1 KB** with Brotli instead of 172 KB uncompressed, a **94%** reduction).
- **`IndexedDB` cache architecture (`db.js`)**: Migrated from the old `localStorage` to asynchronous local storage for the 6–8 MB of Enka game dictionaries (`avatars`, `locs`, `relics`, `weapons`, `namecards`, `pfps`). The cache is reliably kept for 24h with TTL management.
- **`preconnect` and `dns-prefetch` network hints**: Early DNS resolution and TLS handshakes with Enka (`enka.network`) and CDN domains.
- **Optimized image rendering attributes**: Rolled out `decoding="async"` and `loading="lazy"` on showcase visuals, with `fetchpriority="high"` targeted at the main artwork.
- **Automatic XML Sitemap generation (`@astrojs/sitemap`)**: Automated production of `sitemap-index.xml` on every build.
- **Splash art preloading and conversion**: When a player profile loads, the first showcased character's splash art is loaded with priority while the other showcased characters' splash arts load in the background for near-instant display. Splash arts are converted to Lossless WebP and pre-rendered with GPU decoding.

## Changes

- **Dynamic decoupling of `rollTable.json` (~175 KB)**: Now loaded on demand via dynamic ESM import (`import()`), only when scoring or the showcase is used. The initial JavaScript bundle for static pages drops from **~170 KB to 74 KB** (a **55%+** reduction).
- **Lazy-loading of the image export module (`dom-to-image-more`)**: The ~26 KB PNG capture library is now downloaded only when the user clicks the export button.
- **Removed the FontAwesome CDN (~200 KB)**: Replaced the entire external library with lightweight, inlined SVG icons, removing a render-blocking request and lightening the initial load.

# Other

- **Migration to the Astro 5 architecture**: Modular rewrite of the source code into decoupled components (`src/components/`, `src/layouts/`, `src/scripts/`) with centralized state management via Nanostores (`appStore.js`).
- **SEO & advanced search optimization**:
    - Automatic canonical `<link rel="canonical">` tags added to every page.
    - Implemented Schema.org structured data (JSON-LD): `WebApplication` (home), `AboutPage` & `FAQPage` (about), `ProfilePage` & `Organization` (team), and `WebPage` (privacy).

# Fixes

- **Browser memory management**: Permanently eliminated `QuotaExceededError` / "localStorage full" errors and warnings.
- **Script stability**: Fixed a Nanostores import regression (`ReferenceError: setUserData is not defined`) and a variable scope collision (`i`) in event handlers.
- **Alternate theme skeletons**: Fixed an issue where the background (`--bg-card`) and border (`--border-color`) variables stayed locked to the default theme's dark gray for every other theme (Guild, Abyssal, Sea, Wish). Loading animations now properly adapt to the colors of the selected theme.
